READ THE WORK SUBSTACK ABOUT 01 THE WRITER A life of accumulation — now being distilled. I came to poetry the way one comes to stillness: gradually, then all at once. After years of doing and building and becoming, I found myself with something rarer — time. Time to sit with a thought until it became a line. Time to notice the quality of light in October. Time to ask what I actually believe. My work circles the threshold years: that season when you are still fully alive but aware, for the first time, that life has a direction. The light is different here. So is the attention. I write from a place of comfort and curiosity, which I try never to take for granted. I am working toward publication in literary journals, and in the meantime, I share freely on Substack — drafts, fragments, finished pieces, and the thoughts that don't yet know what they are.
